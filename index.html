About Us

Welcome to Danny's Pizzas! We are a family-owned pizzeria that has been serving the community for over 20 years. Our mission is to provide the best pizza in town, made with fresh ingredients and delivered with a smile. Our goal is to exceed your expectations with every bite.

At Danny's Pizzas, we believe that great pizza starts with great ingredients and a passion for perfection. Thats's why we source only the finest materials and pay attention to every detail. Our crust is made fresh daily using traditional methods. And our vegetables are sourced from local farms. We use no artificial ingredients.

Thank you for choosing Danny's Pizzas. We look forward to serving you!
